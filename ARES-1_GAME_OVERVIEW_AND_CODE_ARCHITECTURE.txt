1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & GAMEPLAY GOAL
================================================================================
ARES-1 is an interactive, browser-based 3D Mars base builder and survival simulation
set in Utopia Planitia, Mars. Rendered as a tabletop diorama with tactile,
aerospace-grade models, the player oversees the establishment, growth, and survival
of humanity's first permanent Martian outpost.

Core Objective:
Grow the initial settlement of 6 pioneers up to a thriving, self-sufficient colony
of 50 colonists. To succeed, life support grids (Power, Oxygen, Water, and Food)
must maintain positive net rates per Sol, modules must maintain structural connection
to the base core, and the colony must endure periodic Martian dust storms that cripple
solar generation.

Design Philosophy:
- Zero external build dependencies (single self-contained HTML file).
- Clean, technical aerospace typography (monospace glyphs, chemical notation, amber UI).
- Real-time physical materials (PBR standard materials, bloom, procedural regolith).
- Pure procedural audio (harmonic sine/triangle/sawtooth tone synthesis).

2. ENGINE & RENDERING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
- Three.js Version: 0.164.1 loaded via native browser <script type="importmap">
  pointing to jsdelivr CDN.
- Canvas & Viewport:
  - Responsive WebGLRenderer with high-performance power preference.
  - Pixel ratio capped at min(devicePixelRatio, 2) for smooth 60fps on retina displays.
  - ACESFilmicToneMapping calibrated with toneMappingExposure = 1.25 for crisp
    dynamic range without overexposure glare or murky shadows.
  - PCFSoftShadowMap enabled for soft contact shadows under base modules.
- Lighting Pipeline:
  - Directional Sun Light (3.5 intensity, daylight color #fff3e2): orbits with the
    solar cycle, drops by 60% during dust storms.
  - Hemisphere Light (1.55 intensity, sky #ffdfcb, ground #68382c): simulates diffuse
    Mars atmospheric scatter and red bounce.
  - Fill Directional Light (0.52 intensity, cool tint #d4e2ff): softens hard shadows.
  - Underside Ground Bounce Light (0.28 intensity, warm ochre #de7a50): illuminates
    building foundations and bottom bevels.
  - PMREMGenerator & RoomEnvironment: generates image-based ambient reflections
    (environmentIntensity = 0.62) to give brushed titanium, solar cells, and composite
    plastics realistic specular highlights.
- Post-Processing:
  - EffectComposer with RenderPass and UnrealBloomPass (resolution: innerWidth/innerHeight,
    strength: 0.35, radius: 0.55, threshold: 0.94).
  - Emissive window glass, cyan reactor conduits, antenna beacons, and rover headlights
    emit a distinct, luminous sci-fi bloom.
- Procedural Terrain:
  - PlaneGeometry (112 x 96 units, 112 x 96 subdivisions).
  - Heightmap computed mathematically via Fractional Brownian Motion (fBm) layered with
    perlin noise, crater depressions, and smoothed dune gradients.
  - Custom canvas-generated 256x256 regolith texture applied as diffuse and bump map
    with 8x8 anisotropic repeat.
  - Exposed floating rock slab with 4 sedimentary strata bands around the perimeter.

3. SIMULATION LOOP & RESOURCE ECONOMY
================================================================================
The simulation updates continuously via requestAnimationFrame with a delta time clamp:

Sol Day/Night Cycle:
- 1 Martian Sol = 150 seconds real-time (DAY_SECONDS = 150).
- State phase tracks solar angle:
  phase = (simTime / DAY_SECONDS) % 1.0;
  Sol counter increments every completed revolution.
- daylight() returns a smoothed [0, 1] coefficient based on the sun's elevation.

Resource Economy State (`state.res` & `state.rates`):
1. Power (kW):
   - Generated by Solar Arrays (daylight-dependent) and Nuclear Reactors (24/7 continuous).
   - Consumed by Habitats, Greenhouses, ISRU plants, Drills, and Radar.
   - Deficits draw from Battery Stations. If batteries reach 0 kW, brownout occurs.
2. Oxygen (O₂):
   - Produced by Oxygen ISRU Plants (+32/sol) and CO₂ Sublimators (+8/sol).
   - Consumed by living colonists (-1.5 O₂ per colonist/sol).
3. Water (H₂O):
   - Extracted by Water Ice Drills (+20 liquid water/sol).
   - Consumed by colonists (-1.0/sol), Greenhouses (-3/sol), and ISRU plants (-1/sol).
4. Food:
   - Harvested from Greenhouse Domes (+24 food/sol).
   - Consumed by colonists (-1.2 rations per colonist/sol).
5. Structural Materials:
   - Extracted by Iron Ore Drills (+6/sol), Research Labs (+0.8/sol), or Supply Rockets.
   - Spent to construct and repair modules.
6. Subsurface Ores (Raw Extraction System):
   - Water Ice (waterIce): Subsurface glacial extraction (18/sol per Water Drill).
   - Iron Ore (ironOre): Hematite/magnetite regolith excavation (16/sol per Iron Drill).
   - Carbon Dioxide (co2): Regolith gas desorption & sublimation (22/sol per CO₂ unit).

Colonist Growth Mechanics:
- When berths > pop, and O₂, Water, Food, and Power remain positive (> 0),
  the base gains population growth progress. Once full, a new colonist arrives.
- If resources hit 0, colonists suffer morale and health penalties.

5. THE 5 ENHANCED SYSTEMS
================================================================================

5.1 Subsurface Resource Extraction System:
- Real-time ore extraction counters displayed directly beneath the primary HUD:
  [ W↓ WATER ICE ] [ Fe↓ IRON ORE ] [ CO₂ GAS ]
- Live extraction rates calculated in calculateRates() based on active drills.
- Provides materials and resources essential for sustaining large populations.

5.2 Colonist AI & Real-Time Telemetry:
- 4 Named Initial Pioneers with unique specialized roles and suit designations:
  1. Cmdr. Elena Vance - Mission Commander (Suit: Aerospace Orange)
  2. Dr. Marcus Chen - Chief Geologist (Suit: Cyan Life-Support)
  3. Eng. Tarek Rossi - Systems Engineer (Suit: High-Vis Yellow)
  4. Dr. Aiko Tanaka - Lead Botanist (Suit: Pure White Composite)
- Real-time Telemetry Bars:
  - HP: Physical health (drops during decompression, famine, or extreme cold).
  - O₂: Individual suit oxygen reserve.
  - MOR: Morale (boosted by food diversity and operational habitats; drops in storms).
- Dynamic 3D Camera Tracking: Clicking any colonist card in the Crew Manifest
  smoothly pans and locks the 3D orbital camera directly onto that astronaut
  as they move across the Martian terrain.

5.3 Martian Dust Storm Dynamic Weather System:
- Automated storm timer triggers every 5–10 minutes of simulation time.
- Atmospheric Shader Response:
  - Mars sky top color darkens to dusky umber (#6b443a).
  - Horizon shifts to dense, particulate rust-orange (#9d5e41).
  - Fog distance shrinks, creating a claustrophobic dust haze.
- Solar Suppression: Solar array power output drops immediately by 60%.
- Warning Alert Banner: High-priority technical alert banner slides into view:
  "[ ALERT ] MARTIAN DUST STORM · SOLAR POWER -60% · TAKE COVER"
- Audio Synth: Procedural wind noise gain increases from 0.04 to 0.32.

5.4 Nuclear Power Grid & Brownout Hazard Glow:
- Nuclear Fission Reactor produces 95 kW continuous baseload energy unaffected
  by dust storms or day/night cycles.
- Power Grid Validation Logic:
  - Modules check whether base stored power > 0 and whether they are connected
    to the active grid.
  - Disconnected buildings or modules operating during an electrical brownout
    have their status indicator lights switched to pulsing red hazard beacons
    (MAT.redLight), giving instant visual cues across the base.

5.5 Step-by-Step Mission Directives System:
- Persistent HUD panel displaying 5 progressive mission objectives:
  1. "1. Establish Initial Colony": Build Habitat & Solar Array.
  2. "2. Subsurface Mining Rig": Deploy Water Ice or Iron Ore Drill.
  3. "3. Life Support Synthesis": Deploy Oxygen ISRU Plant & Greenhouse Dome.
  4. "4. Nuclear Power Grid": Deploy a Nuclear Fission Reactor.
  5. "5. Deep Space Communications": Deploy Radar Comms Dish & reach 20+ colonists.
- Real-time progress bar fills automatically as prerequisites are completed.

6. CONTROLS, CAMERA PRESETS & SURFACE WALK MODE
================================================================================
Camera Navigation:
- Left-click + drag: Orbit camera around focal point.
- Right-click + drag: Pan across the Utopia Planitia terrain.
- Scroll wheel: Zoom in/out (distance clamped between 12 and 190 units).
- Number Keys 1–5: Instant camera preset angles:
  [1] Overview High Orbit (Default diorama view)
  [2] Habitat Hub Low Angle (Close-up of base center)
  [3] Mining & Extraction Ridge
  [4] Solar Array & Power Field
  [5] Top-Down Blueprint Satellite View

Construction Controls:
- Click card in bottom build tray: Select building for deployment.
- Hover terrain: Displays green hologram ghost (valid) or red ghost (blocked/slope).
- R: Rotate active module by 90°.
- Left-click terrain: Deploy module and deduct materials.
- X: Deconstruct selected module (recovers 50% materials).
- Ctrl + Z / Ctrl + Y: Full Undo and Redo deployment stack.
- Spacebar: Pause / Resume simulation clock.

Surface Walk Mode (First-Person Astronaut):
- Toggle with "F" key or the "WALK" button in top toolbar.
- Controls: W/A/S/D to walk across terrain, Shift to sprint.
- Mouse movement pitches and yaws the helmet visor.
- Camera height dynamically tracks terrainHeight(x, z) + 1.72m eye level.

Fullscreen & UI Controls:
- "BACK TO COSMOS": Top-left button navigates back to the Cosmos Magazine portal.
- "EXIT FULLSCREEN": Positioned cleanly directly beneath the Back button (left: 16px).
- "GUIDE & MISSIONS": Persistent glowing amber button anchored in the bottom-right corner.

7. STEP-BY-STEP MISSION COMPLETION WALKTHROUGH
================================================================================
Follow this optimal operational order to achieve mission victory:

PHASE 1: SURVIVAL FOUNDATION (Directive 1)
- Start: You begin with 320 Materials, 120 Power, and 6 Colonists.
- Step 1: Select Habitat Module (H–01, 60 Mat) and deploy it adjacent to the core.
- Step 2: Select Solar Array (S≡≡, 25 Mat) and deploy nearby.
- Step 3: Use a Connecting Tunnel (T═, 12 Mat) to bridge the living modules.
- Result: Housing berths expand to 16, power generation rises by +32 kW during day.

PHASE 2: SECURING RAW EXTRACTS (Directive 2)
- Step 4: Select Water Ice Drill (W↓, 80 Mat) and deploy on the open regolith.
- Result: Begins drilling 18 ice and 20 water per Sol. Water counter turns positive.

PHASE 3: LIFE SUPPORT INDEPENDENCE (Directive 3)
- Step 5: Select Oxygen ISRU Plant (O₂, 90 Mat) and place near the main base.
  Generates +32 Oxygen/sol, securing breathable air for up to 21 colonists.
- Step 6: Select Greenhouse Dome (G◒, 75 Mat) and deploy.
  Uses 3 water to produce 24 food/sol. Colonist famine risk is eliminated.

PHASE 4: DUST STORM DEFENSE & BASELOAD POWER (Directive 4)
- Step 7: Deploy a Battery Station (B▥, 55 Mat) to buffer night power.
- Step 8: Save 150 materials (accumulated via Iron Ore Drills or supply rocket drops).
- Step 9: Deploy the Nuclear Reactor (N⊛, 150 Mat).
  Generates 95 kW continuous power 24/7. Dust storm alerts will no longer brown out
  your life-support grid!

PHASE 5: EXPANSION & VICTORY (Directive 5)
- Step 10: Deploy Radar / Comms Dish (C◝, 70 Mat) to initiate orbital link.
- Step 11: Deploy 3 additional Habitat Modules (H–01) to expand berths past 24.
- Step 12: Ensure all rates (Power, O₂, Water, Food) remain green (+).
- Step 13: Watch supply rockets land at the Landing Pad with new colonists until
  the population reaches 50 pioneers.
- Result: Victory screen triggers: "MISSION COMPLETE: A NEW WORLD, INHABITED."
