ARES-1 · BASE OPERATIONS

Grow a self-sufficient settlement to 50 colonists. Habitats only provide berths when connected to the core by airlocks or tunnels. In Sandbox, construction is free and the crew cannot be lost.

Construction

  • Choose a module below. Move the ghost over the slab; green means valid, red means blocked. Click or tap to deploy.
  • R rotates · X deletes the selected module · Ctrl Z / Ctrl Y undo / redo.
  • Connect living modules with short airlocks or tunnel pieces. Watch oxygen, food, water, and stored power.

Camera & surface

  • Left-drag orbits, right-drag pans, wheel zooms. Double-click resets. Number keys 1–5 select viewpoints.
  • F enters surface walk. Use WASD, drag to look, and Shift to sprint. Touch devices get direction buttons.

Operations

  • Click any module for telemetry and actions. Landing pads call supply rockets; garages dispatch rovers; radar stations launch survey drones.
  • Dust storms suppress solar output. Batteries keep life support alive. Meteors and failures damage modules; repair them from their info panel.
  • Space pauses. Save / load uses this browser’s localStorage only; it does not sync between devices.
