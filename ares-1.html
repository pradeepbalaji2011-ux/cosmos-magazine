ARES-1 MISSION MANUAL

SURFACE OPERATIONS · COLONY SIMULATION · DIRECTIVES GUIDE

COLONY OBJECTIVE

Grow a resilient, self-sufficient Martian settlement from 6 initial crew up to 50 colonists. Maintain positive reserves of Power, Oxygen, Water, Food, and Materials while defending against harsh Martian weather events.

CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS

  • Left Drag Orbit camera diorama
  • Right Drag Pan across Utopia Planitia
  • Mouse Wheel Zoom camera in / out
  • Click Module Select module for info & telemetry
  • R Rotate deployment ghost by 90°
  • X Delete selected module (50% refund)
  • Ctrl + Z / Y Undo / Redo base edits
  • F First-person Surface Walk (WASD + mouse)
  • Space Pause / resume simulation
  • 1 – 5 Camera viewpoint presets

POWER GRID & DUST STORMS

  • Solar Arrays (S≡≡): Generate 32 power during sunlight, 0 at night. Output drops by 60% during Martian Dust Storms.
  • Nuclear Reactor (N⊛): Generates a massive 95 continuous power 24/7, immune to daylight loss and dust storms.
  • Battery Station (B▥): Stores 180 units of emergency power to keep life support humming overnight.
  • Disconnected Warning: Buildings without power or unlinked from the grid will flash warning red lights.

RESOURCE EXTRACTION DRILLS

  • Water Ice Drill (W↓): Extracts subsurface polar permafrost (+18 ice/sol & +20 liquid water).
  • Iron Ore Drill (Fe↓): Excavates regolith deposits for smelting into structural construction materials (+16 ore/sol & +6 materials).
  • CO₂ Sublimator (CO₂): Compresses Martian atmosphere (+22 CO₂/sol & +8 oxygen) to feed greenhouses and life support.

COLONIST AI & CREW MANIFEST

Monitor your 4 expedition pioneers in the bottom-left panel (Elena Vance, Dr. Marcus Chen, Tarek Rossi, Dr. Aiko Tanaka). Keep their Health (HP), Oxygen (O₂), and Morale (MOR) full. Click on any colonist card to dynamically follow them in real-time 3D as they walk between habitats and workstations.

STEP-BY-STEP MISSION OBJECTIVES

Complete all 5 core directives to establish permanent human presence on Mars:

01 ESTABLISH INITIAL COLONY FOUNDATION

Task: Deploy at least 1 Habitat Module (H–01) and 1 Solar Array (S≡≡).

Walkthrough: Click the Habitat module card (60 materials) in the bottom build tray, position it on the flat slab, and click to place. Then select Solar array (25 materials) and place it nearby. Connect them using a short Connecting tunnel (T═) or place them adjacent so pressure and power link.

02 SUBSURFACE DRILLING RIG EXTRACTION

Task: Deploy a Water Ice Drill (W↓) or Iron Ore Drill (Fe↓).

Walkthrough: Select the Water ice drill (80 materials) in the build bar and deploy it on the red terrain. It consumes 8 power to extract 18 water ice per Sol, securing foundational fluids for life support.

03 LIFE SUPPORT SYNTHESIS ATMOSPHERE & FOOD

Task: Build an Oxygen ISRU Plant (O₂) and a Greenhouse Dome (G◒).

Walkthrough: Deploy the Oxygen / ISRU plant (90 materials) to generate 32 breathable oxygen from the atmosphere. Next, deploy the Greenhouse dome (75 materials) which uses water and power to grow 24 fresh rations per Sol, ensuring colonists do not starve.

04 NUCLEAR POWER GRID ENERGY INDEPENDENCE

Task: Deploy a Nuclear Fission Reactor (N⊛).

Walkthrough: Accumulate 150 materials (manufactured by mining drills or supply rockets). Select Nuclear reactor and position the heavy dual-tower fission core. Once deployed, it provides 95 reliable power 24 hours a day, shielding your base from dust storm blackouts.

05 DEEP SPACE COMMUNICATIONS & EXPANSION MISSION ACCOMPLISHED

Task: Deploy a Radar / Comms Dish (C◝) and grow base berths to reach 20+ colonists.

Walkthrough: Build the Radar / comms dish (70 materials) to initiate interplanetary beacon synchronization with Earth. Expand living space by adding 2–3 more Habitat Modules. With life support, food, and power secured, new colonists will arrive via supply rockets until you reach full colonization!
